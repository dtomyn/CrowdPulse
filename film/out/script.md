`0:26` `create-3` Choose an accent colour, switch on the features you want, list it on the home page if you like, and create it.

`0:32` `create-4` You instantly get a QR code and a six-character join code.

## 2:12 Read the room

**Shows:** Control room telemetry: gauge, distribution, KPIs, chapter markers, mood chart and the presenter coach

**Camera:** Control room full; zooms on the gauge, KPIs, the chart and the coach alert

**Sound:** Clicks

`2:21` `room-2` Drop chapter markers as you move through your talk, so later you can see which parts landed.

`2:27` `room-3` And a built-in coach nudges you when the mood dips, or spikes.

## 2:32 Passive signals

**Shows:** Tab focus, the opt-in attendee camera and its consent sheet, the control room Passive signals card, said vs. shown, and the distracted coach nudge

**Camera:** Phone close-up on the consent sheet, then the control room card, the said-vs-shown meters, the coach alert and the dashed attention line

**Sound:** Clicks

`2:32` `passive-1` CrowdPulse can also read the room passively. When the host allows it, attendees can choose to share their attention.

`2:40` `passive-2` Their camera is analysed on their own phone. No video ever leaves it: just three numbers, for attention, positive, and fatigue.

`2:49` `passive-3` In the control room, the passive signals card blends tab focus, cameras and a room sensor into one attention index, shown only once five or more people take part.

`3:00` `passive-4` It also compares what people say with what they show. When ratings stay high but attention slips, the coach flags it: polite, but distracted.

`3:53` `report-3` Then engagement, reactions, sentiment, a word cloud, and the Net Promoter Score.

`4:02` `report-4` Export it as a PDF, a CSV or JSON, and share it with your audience if you like.
