How to Get Live Audience Feedback While You Present (Full Walkthrough)

DESCRIPTION
What if you could see how your talk is landing while you give it?

CrowdPulse lets your audience rate your session live from their phones. You see the room's mood second by second, and when the lights come up you get a full report. This 3-minute walkthrough goes from creating a session to that final report.

What you'll see:
• Create a session with a title, time and location: in person, virtual or hybrid
• Get a QR code and 6-character join code. No app and no sign-up for attendees
• 8 ways to rate: slider, smileys, stars, a rotary dial, weather, a thermometer, thumbs and vibes
• Emoji reaction storms that float across every screen, including the projector
• Live chat, Q&A with upvotes, instant polls, and spotlighting a question on every screen
• A control room with a live mood gauge, rating spread, energy, trend and chapter markers
• A built-in coach that nudges you when the mood dips or spikes
• End-of-session feedback: stars, Net Promoter Score and one-word reviews
• A post-session report: grade, mood timeline, key moments, engagement funnel, word cloud, sentiment, NPS, plus PDF, CSV and JSON export

Get started: run npm start, then open localhost:3000/admin. Attendees on the same Wi-Fi join by scanning the QR code.

Everything in this video is the real application, recorded frame by frame. The audience in the demo is simulated, and the narration uses a synthetic voice (Kokoro text-to-speech).
