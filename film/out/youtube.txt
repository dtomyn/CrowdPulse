DESCRIPTION
Your audience rates your talk five stars. But were they actually paying attention?

CrowdPulse shows you both, live. Attendees rate your session from their phones while it happens, and passive signals show what the room is doing without anyone pressing a button. When the lights come up, you get a report that lines up what people said with what they showed.

This 4-minute walkthrough covers everything, from creating a session to the final report.

What you'll see:
• Create a session in seconds: in person, virtual or hybrid, with a QR code and a 6-character join code
• NEW: list a session on the home page under "Happening now", so people can find it without a code
• Attendees join from any phone, with no app and no sign-up
• 9 ways to rate: slider, smileys, stars, a rotary dial, weather, a thermometer, thumbs, vibes and (NEW) tilting your phone like a spirit level
• Emoji reaction storms that float across every screen, including the projector
• Live chat, Q&A with upvotes, instant polls, and spotlighting a question on every screen
• A control room with a live mood gauge, chapter markers and a coach that nudges you when the mood shifts
• NEW: passive signals. Tab focus, opt-in attendee cameras and a room sensor combine into one attention index
• NEW: "said vs. shown". When ratings stay high but attention drops, the coach flags it: polite, but distracted
• A post-session report with a grade, mood timeline, key moments, rating vs. attention, laughter and applause peaks, word cloud, NPS, and PDF, CSV and JSON export

Privacy by design: camera analysis runs on each attendee's own device and is strictly opt-in. Only three numbers are sent (attention, positive, fatigue). No video, images or audio ever leave the device, and crowd figures only appear once at least 5 people contribute.
